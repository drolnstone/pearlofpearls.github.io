Since 2015

Birthed in Calabar. Growing in Liverpool.

Pearl of Pearls was launched on 26 April 2015 in Calabar, Nigeria, out of a simple concern: teenage girls were growing up without the godly mentorship that helps a young woman understand who she is and what she carries.

What began as one gathering became a movement. Over ten years, Pearl of Pearls has grown to three branches and reached more than a thousand young people through mentoring, conferences, outreaches and skills training. The girls we started with are women now, and some of them mentor the next ones.

The Liverpool branch is our newest, and it is where this next chapter is being written.

Who we are for

The holistic care of the girl child.

We are here for girls in their teenage years, roughly 12 to 19: the age when a young woman is deciding what she is worth, and often deciding it on somebody else's terms. Younger sisters who come along with them are welcome too.

Our aim is that she is inspired, groomed and equipped to reach her own dreams, and that she grows into a woman who lives with confidence and security wherever she finds herself.

Inspire

To lift her sights beyond mediocrity and show her what is possible.

Encourage

To give her the boldness to face the challenges that threaten the girl child.

Equip

To hand her knowledge and skills she can use in every sphere of life.

Our annual conference

INSPIRE HER

Once a year, women of every age come into one room: schoolgirls, students, mothers, professionals, grandmothers. INSPIRE HER is a woman to woman mentorship conference built to close the gap between the generations.

Girls hear from women who have already walked the road they are on, and ask the questions they cannot easily ask anywhere else. It has run since the earliest years of Pearl of Pearls and remains the moment the whole movement looks forward to.

  • Open to females of all ages, whether or not they attend our other gatherings
  • Mentorship from women established in their fields, during and after the sessions
  • Practical skills sessions alongside the talks
  • Free to attend

For parents and carers

Before you bring your daughter.

What actually happens?

A gathering is conversation, food, a short talk or Bible study, and time to be together. Girls are never put on the spot, and nobody has to speak or pray aloud.

Who will be with her?

Vetted adult volunteers, never fewer than two. Everyone working with our girls is DBS checked and works to our code of conduct.

How do you keep her safe?

We have a written safeguarding and child protection policy with a named safeguarding lead. Ask us for a copy at any time and we will send it.

Does she have to be a Christian?

No. We are openly Christian and we say so, but every girl is welcome exactly as she is, and no one is pressed to believe anything.

Is my daughter the right age?

Our gatherings are built around girls aged 12 to 19. If you have a younger daughter who would come along with an older sister, bring her; just stay with her while she is with us.

What will it cost?

Nothing. Our activities are free, and they always have been.

Can I stay or visit first?

Yes, and we would rather you did. Come and see for yourself, meet the mentors, and decide afterwards.
